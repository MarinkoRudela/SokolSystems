  7. The playbook 3

    Refresh before it burns out

    When frequency climbs and your cost per result starts creeping up, the ad is getting tired. Make new versions of it before results slide further.

  8. The playbook 4

    Show the product early, in use

    Get it on screen in the first seconds, ideally in someone’s hands. People buy what they can picture themselves using.

    Product on screen
  9. The playbook 5

    Design for sound off

    Plenty of people scroll with the sound off. Put the message in captions and on-screen text so the ad works either way.

    They arrived. First run tomorrow.

  10. The playbook 6

    Make every size, mind the edges

    4:5 for feeds, 9:16 for Reels and Stories. In vertical video, keep text away from the top, the bottom and the right edge, where the app’s buttons sit.

    App header Caption and buttons Keep text here
  11. The playbook 7

    Mix your formats

    Static, video, carousel and UGC-style catch people in different moods. A winning angle often wins again in a new format.

  12. The playbook 8

    Let the numbers pick the champion

    Your favorite isn’t always the winner. Run versions side by side, keep what converts, send it back to us, and go again.

Questions

Good to know.

How does it work?

Send us an ad or post that’s performing well. We work out why it works and make new versions built on it: new hooks, angles, formats and sizes. You test them, keep the best, and send the new winners back. Then we go again.

Do you need access to my ad account?

No. You know which ads and posts are performing. Send us the winners as a file or a link (a screenshot of the results helps) and we take it from there.

Does it work for social posts too?

Yes. If an organic post or reel got strong engagement, send it over and we’ll make more in that style, format and hook, for any platform.

What if I don’t have a winner yet?

Then we start from your product, your brand and what’s working in your category, and you send us your first winner once it shows up. You can always request something brand new.

Can you guarantee my ads will perform?

No one honestly can. What we promise is the process: every round builds on what your audience has already responded to, delivered within 48 hours.

How is it unlimited?

Add as many requests as you like, with no monthly cap. We produce them one at a time, and each one is delivered within 48 hours of when we start it, so there's always work moving.

Is it only ads?

No. Ads and winners are the core, but the same subscription covers product shots, lifestyle and editorial photography, UGC-style content, action shots, social posts and edited Reels and TikToks. Send raw footage and we’ll cut it, or send a video that’s working and we’ll remix it. If your brand needs it in a feed, send it as a request.

What can you make from a winner?

New hooks and openings, new angles, the same idea in other formats (static, video, carousel, UGC-style, edited Reels), fresh visuals, and every size you need, for ads and for social posts.

How fast is delivery?

Within 48 hours, guaranteed, from static ads to UGC-style videos. The clock starts when we begin your request (it moves to In progress in your portal) and only counts Monday to Friday, US Eastern Time.

What if I don't like something?

Tell us what to change and we revise it until it's right. Revisions are unlimited.

How can it cost this little?

A human creative director owns the strategy and quality of every piece, while our AI production system removes the slow, expensive parts of traditional production: crews, shoots and long approval chains.

Do I own the work?

Yes. Everything we deliver is yours to use in ads, on your site and on social media.

Is there a contract?

No. It's month to month. Pause or cancel in one click from your billing portal.
