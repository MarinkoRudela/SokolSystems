Part 1 Before you start

1

Get your accounts ready

About 15 min

  1. A paid Claude plan and the Claude desktop app (download it from claude.com/download and sign in).
  2. A Google account with Drive and Gmail. A normal Gmail account works; Google Workspace is fine too.
  3. An Airtable account. The free plan is enough to start.
  4. A paid Higgsfield plan, for making images and video: higgsfield.ai. Affiliate link: Sokol earns a commission if you buy a plan, at no extra cost to you.
  5. Meta Ads Manager access, if you run ads. Optional: Claude can also start from your product and best posts.

You should see: Four accounts you can sign in to. Write down which Google account you’ll use: the kit folder is shared with it.

Stuck?
Which Claude plan do I need?
Any paid plan works for setup. If you make lots of creative each week, a higher plan gives you more room before you hit usage limits.
Do I have to use Higgsfield?
For “make the next request”, yes: the recipes are written and tested for it. Everything else (briefs, hooks, ad copy, reports) works without it.
2

Turn on file creation in Claude

About 2 min

  1. In Claude, open Settings > Capabilities.
  2. Turn on Create and edit files (on some accounts it’s called Code execution and file creation).

You should see: The switch is on. Skills only work with it on.

Stuck?
I can’t find the setting.
You’re probably on a Team or Enterprise plan. Your admin turns it on in Organization settings > Plugins & skills, on the Policy tab, together with Skills.
3

Open your kit folder

About 2 min

  1. Open the kit folder (also in your welcome email) while signed in to the Google account it was shared with.
  2. Have a look, but don’t copy, move or rename anything. Claude copies what you need into your own Drive during setup.

You should see: A folder called AI Creative System Kit with setup, commands, templates, prompts, updates, install and START-HERE.

Stuck?
Google says “You need access”.
You’re signed in to a different Google account. Switch accounts (your profile picture, top right), or reply to your welcome email with the account you want to use and we’ll move the access.

Part 2 Install and connect

4

Install the skill

About 5 min

  1. Download creative-system.zip (it’s also in the kit folder’s install folder). Don’t unzip it.
  2. In Claude, go to Customize > Skills.
  3. Click +, then + Create skill, then Upload a skill, and choose the zip.
  4. Make sure the switch next to creative-system is on.

You should see: creative-system in your skills list, switched on.

Stuck?
The upload fails.
Check the file is still a .zip. Safari on a Mac can unzip downloads by itself: download it again with Chrome, or right-click the unzipped folder and choose Compress. Also check file creation is on (step 2).
I don’t see Customize.
Update the Claude desktop app to the latest version, or try claude.ai in your browser.
5

Connect Google Drive and Gmail

About 5 min

  1. In a new chat, click + (bottom left), hover over Connectors and choose Manage connectors, then Browse connectors.
  2. Find Google Drive, click Connect and sign in with the same Google account your kit folder is shared with. Allow access.
  3. Do the same for Gmail.

You should see: Google Drive and Gmail listed as connected.

Stuck?
I connected the wrong Google account.
Disconnect it in the same connectors list and connect again with the right account.
6

Connect Airtable

About 3 min

  1. In the same connectors list, find Airtable and click Connect.
  2. When Airtable asks what Claude can use, give it access to the workspace where you want your system, including future bases. Claude creates a new base there during setup.

You should see: Airtable listed as connected.

Stuck?
Claude says it can’t create a base.
Reconnect Airtable and include the whole workspace (not just existing bases).
7

Connect Higgsfield

About 3 min

  1. In Claude, open Settings > Connectors and click Add custom connector.
  2. Paste https://mcp.higgsfield.ai/mcp and click Connect.
  3. Sign in to Higgsfield and allow access.

You should see: Higgsfield listed in your connectors.

Stuck?
It connects but nothing generates.
Higgsfield needs a paid plan with credits. Check your balance at higgsfield.ai.
8

Start the right chat

About 1 min

  1. Open a new chat in the Claude desktop app.
  2. In the model menu, pick the most capable model on your plan (currently Claude Opus 5.5).
  3. Click + > Connectors and check Google Drive, Gmail, Airtable and Higgsfield are switched on for this chat.

You should see: A fresh chat with the best model and all four connectors on.

Part 3 Set me up

9

Type “set me up”

About 40 min

  1. Type set me up and send it.
  2. Claude checks your connections, then asks for your Buyer ID (in your welcome email), your business name, website, what you sell, where you advertise and the email for notifications.
  3. It builds your folders in Drive, your Airtable base, your brand profile and your email alerts, telling you which stage it’s on. Click Allow when it asks to use a tool.
  4. Keep the chat open until it says it’s finished. It takes 20 to 40 minutes.

You should see: A Creative System folder in your Drive, a Creative System base in Airtable, a Brand profile doc, and a short finish list from Claude.

Stuck?
Claude can’t find the kit folder.
Paste the kit folder link from your welcome email into the chat.
Claude stopped halfway.
Type continue. If the chat ran out of room, start a new chat and type monthly check-up: it checks what was built and tells you what’s missing.
A connector isn’t working.
Go back to steps 5 to 7, reconnect it, then type continue.
10

Check your brand profile

About 15 min

  1. Open Creative System > Brand > Brand profile in your Drive.
  2. Fix anything that’s wrong, especially lines marked check this: products, prices, tone, and claims you must never make.

You should see: A brand profile you’d be happy to hand to a designer. Claude reads it before every piece of work.

Stuck?
It got something badly wrong.
Tell Claude what’s wrong in a chat and type build my brand profile to redo it.
11

Finish the last manual steps

About 15 min

  1. In Airtable, open the Creative System base, go to Automations and switch on the three automations. Claude can’t switch them on for you.
  2. Add your logo and 5 to 10 product photos to Creative System > Brand in Drive.
  3. In the Airtable Assets table, tick Rights confirmed only for files you own or have permission to use in ads.

You should see: Everything on Claude’s finish list ticked off.

Stuck?
There are no automations in Airtable.
Claude gives you click-by-click steps in its finish list instead. Follow those, or ask it: “show me how to create the Airtable automations”.

Part 4 Your first ad

12

Find your winners

About 20 min

  1. Type find my winners.
  2. If Claude can’t read your ads directly, it asks for an export from Ads Manager (or screenshots) with: ad name, amount spent, results, cost per result, CTR and frequency.
  3. Tell it what a result is for you (a purchase, a lead, a booking).

You should see: Your ads sorted into winners, promising, below average and not enough data, saved in the Winners table.

Stuck?
I don’t run ads yet.
Skip this. Claude can start from your product and your best organic posts.
13

Break it down and brief it

About 20 min

  1. Type break down this ad and pick your best winner. Claude explains the hook, angle, format and offer that made it work.
  2. Type brief 5 variations of this. Each variation changes one thing, so your results tell you something.

You should see: A new request in your Airtable Requests table with a clear brief.

14

Make the next request

About 30 min

  1. Type make the next request.
  2. Claude shows its plan and the estimated Higgsfield credits. Nothing is generated until you say yes.
  3. It makes quick drafts first. Pick the best direction, then it makes the finished versions at high quality.

You should see: New files in Creative System > Deliveries and the request set to Ready for review.

Stuck?
The label or logo comes out wrong.
Make sure a clear product photo is in Brand with Rights confirmed. The recipes attach it and spell out your label text; type check this delivery to catch problems.
I’m running out of credits.
Ask Claude to make fewer drafts, or top up in Higgsfield.
15

Check, size and approve

About 20 min

  1. Type check this delivery to run the 15-point quality check.
  2. Type make all sizes for 4:5, 9:16 and 1:1 versions.
  3. Type is this allowed? before anything goes live.
  4. Approve it yourself in Airtable. Claude never ticks Approved.

You should see: An approved ad, in every size, ready to launch.

Part 5 Every week

16

Run your weekly rhythm

About 10 min

  1. Monday: weekly report and what’s wearing out?
  2. Tuesday: break down this ad, brief 5 variations of this
  3. Wednesday: make the next request, write 5 hooks for this
  4. Thursday: check this delivery, make all sizes, is this allowed?
  5. Friday: launch, and write ad copy for this
  6. Once a month: monthly check-up

You should see: About 6 to 12 hours a week. The full plan is in Creative System > System files > templates > weekly-rhythm.

Stuck?
No winners this week?
Make new angles on your best product instead of more versions of a weak ad.

All done. Nice work.

Your system is set up and you’ve made your first ad. From here it’s the weekly rhythm: winners in, new versions out.

Keeping your kit current

When AI tools change, we update the kit. Type run the update in Claude: it shows what’s new and installs it without touching your own work. Your included updates last 6 months from purchase (the date is in your welcome email).

After that, the kit keeps working exactly as it is. If you want new updates, the Kit Updates membership is $29 a month or $279 a year, cancel anytime. We’ll email you before your included months end with the link.
