Where jewelry brands get stuck.

  • Small products are hard to shoot

    Reflections, sparkle and tiny details take specialist lighting, and a phone photo rarely does the piece justice.

  • Every piece needs to be seen worn

    People want to see scale and how it looks on, which usually means models and a shoot for each collection.

  • Gifting seasons arrive all at once

    Holidays, Valentine’s Day and Mother’s Day all need fresh creative, while the best-selling ad runs until it tires.
