Where home brands get stuck.

  • Lifestyle shoots are expensive

    Styled rooms, props and locations add up fast, so most brands make do with a handful of scenes.

  • Sale season needs everything at once

    Black Friday and holiday promos need dozens of assets in a few weeks.

  • Big products are hard to explain in one image

    Cookware, furniture and bedding need detail, scale and use, not just a hero shot.
