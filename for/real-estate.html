Ad rules worth knowing

In the US, ads for housing, including listings and open houses, must run under Meta’s Housing special ad category, which limits targeting by age, gender and ZIP code. Fair housing rules apply to the creative too, so we keep it about the home and the area, never about who should live there.

General guidance, not legal advice. Platform rules change, so check Meta’s current Advertising Standards before you run anything.
