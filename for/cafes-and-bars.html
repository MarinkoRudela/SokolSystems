Where cafés and bars get stuck.

  • Every event needs its own post

    Quiz night, live music, a new seasonal drink: each one needs content, every week.

  • Drinks are hard to shoot well

    Ice melts, foam settles and the light in a bar at night is rarely kind to a phone.

  • The feed goes quiet between big nights

    Regulars follow you; new people need a reason to stop scrolling.

How it works

Send a winner. Get champions back.

  1. Send your winner

    Your best-performing ad or post, plus anything we should know: the offer, the season, what’s changed.

  2. We build on it

Ad rules worth knowing

If you serve alcohol, Meta requires US ads for it to target people 21 and over, and ads can’t show excessive drinking or link drinking to risky activities. We build drinks ads with that in mind.

General guidance, not legal advice. Platform rules change, so check Meta’s current Advertising Standards before you run anything.
