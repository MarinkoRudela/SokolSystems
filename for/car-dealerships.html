Ad rules worth knowing

In the US, ads for car loans and financing offers fall under Meta’s Financial products and services category, which limits targeting. Model, service and test-drive ads usually don’t, so it pays to have strong creative for both.

General guidance, not legal advice. Platform rules change, so check Meta’s current Advertising Standards before you run anything.
