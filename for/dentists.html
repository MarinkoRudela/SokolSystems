Ad rules worth knowing

Meta’s ad rules don’t let ads imply they know a viewer’s health or personal traits (‘Missing teeth?’ aimed at the reader). We write to the outcome instead: what the visit feels like, how fast it is to book, what the result looks like.

General guidance, not legal advice. Platform rules change, so check Meta’s current Advertising Standards before you run anything.
