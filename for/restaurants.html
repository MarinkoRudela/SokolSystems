Where restaurants get stuck.

  • The happy-hour ad from three years ago

    It still runs because it once worked. The menu and the room have moved on.

  • No time to shoot between services

    Good food photos need light, styling and time, all in short supply during a dinner rush.

  • Specials that nobody sees in time

    A weekend special needs to be in the feed by Thursday, not the following Tuesday.

Keyword searches tend to surface long-running ads, so treat this as a snapshot rather than an average. We don’t name the businesses. Our earlier study found the same pattern: all 5 restaurants we checked had an ad running for over a year.
