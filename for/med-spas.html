Ad rules worth knowing

Meta’s health and wellness rules require cosmetic-procedure ads to target people 18 and over, and they ban side-by-side before-and-after images for treatments like Botox and fillers. Ads also can’t imply they know something personal about the viewer (‘Tired of your wrinkles?’). We write around these from the start.

General guidance, not legal advice. Platform rules change, so check Meta’s current Advertising Standards before you run anything.

Questions from med spas.

Yes. We build them to Meta’s health and wellness rules: no side-by-side before-and-afters for wrinkle treatments, no copy that points at the viewer’s appearance, and you confirm every claim before it runs.
