1. Who we are

Sokol Systems ("we", "us") provides a subscription creative service producing advertising and marketing creative, including static ads, video ads, UGC-style videos, edited short-form video, product visuals and photography, and social content. By subscribing you agree to these terms.

2. The subscription

The subscription costs $2,995 per month, billed in advance through Stripe. You may submit unlimited requests. Your subscription includes unlimited brands. Requests for all of your brands share one queue and are produced one at a time; the next begins when the current one is delivered. Adding a new brand counts as one request: we use that turn to research the brand and build its profile. We guarantee delivery of each request within 48 hours of starting work on it (when its status changes to In progress). The 48 hours count Monday to Friday, US Eastern Time; weekends and US federal holidays are excluded. Because we take on a limited number of clients, new subscriptions may be waitlisted when we are at capacity.
